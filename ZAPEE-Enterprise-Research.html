Risk Intelligence

3 Things That Can Stop Your Shipment

All three risks are completely preventable — but only if you act before the shipment leaves China.

⛔ Critical Risk
Missing Food and Drug Administration Permits
Toiletries, shampoos, and cleaning products are regulated. If ZAPEE's company permit (License to Operate) or any product permit (Certificate of Product Notification) is not already approved when the shipment arrives at port, the Bureau of Customs holds it — indefinitely. Port storage fees accumulate every day.
How to PreventSecure the License to Operate and a Certificate of Product Notification for every unique product BEFORE placing your bulk order. Start Day 1 — it takes 4–8 weeks.
⛔ Critical Risk
Declaring a Lower Invoice Value Than You Actually Paid
The Bureau of Customs checks every declared value against international price databases. If your declared value is more than 30% below their benchmark, it triggers automatic seizure proceedings. The penalty is up to 500% of the taxes owed — plus possible permanent forfeiture of your entire shipment.
How to PreventAlways declare the true price you paid on the commercial invoice. Never ask your supplier to write a lower value. Keep your Alibaba order records as proof.
⚠️ High Risk
Using the Wrong Product Classification Code
Every imported product has a specific Harmonized System Tariff Code — an 8–10 digit international product number. One wrong digit can mean a higher duty rate, a mandatory physical inspection of your entire shipment, and significant delays at the port.
How to PreventLook up the correct code at finder.tariffcommission.gov.ph before your supplier writes the invoice. The same code must appear on the invoice, packing list, and Certificate of Origin.
⛔ What Happens If Your Shipment Is Seized
Day 1
Warrant of Seizure and Detention issued — cargo placed on hold at the port
Day 7–14
Formal Notice of Seizure delivered to ZAPEE — legal proceedings begin, storage fees accumulate
Day 30–90
Seizure hearing scheduled — ZAPEE must present original invoices to prove the true value
Resolution
Pay all duties, taxes, and surcharges to release OR goods are permanently forfeited
